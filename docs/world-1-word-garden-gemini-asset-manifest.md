## Copy-ready Gemini prompts

**Garden.** Use the attached First Principles World 1 garden as the approved style and lighting reference. Paint a sunlit garden bed from the same elevated angle, with emerald foliage and a single coral and gold focal blossom. Frame it as a shallow horizontal strip that works at phone width. Keep the left third clear for the existing Sprig. Deliver the bed as a clean plate and export bud and open bloom separately on transparency with identical stem anchors. Crisp 2D animated-feature rendering with soft distant atmosphere. No characters, words, interface, paper textures or plastic gloss.

**Waterfall.** Match the attached World 1 river painting. Show a river bend with a low waterfall on the right third and a quiet bank on the left for Sprig. Keep both in a shallow mobile-safe crop. Export the clean terrain separately from the water curtain, surface ripples and splash accents. Register each transparent layer against the same plate coordinates. Use turquoise water, warm sunlight and readable silhouettes. No interface, baked foam or unrelated scenery.

**Bridge.** Match the attached World 1 bridge and creek references. Paint a creek scene with a clear span from the middle to the right and room for Sprig on the left bank. Keep the near shore and destination visible on phones. Export the creek as a clean plate and provide two bridge sections that meet exactly when assembled, plus a separate foreground foliage silhouette. Match the existing perspective and ground contact. No characters, text or baked-in bridge.
